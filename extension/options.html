two settings, and the first one is the one that matters.

the public default rate limits hard, so a check often comes back saying it could not read everything. your own endpoint fixes that. helius, quicknode and triton all have free tiers that are plenty for this.
higher is a wider check and a slower one. 100 is fine on the public endpoint. raise it once you have your own.

this extension sends the mint address of the coin you are looking at to the rpc endpoint above, and nothing else, to nobody else. no analytics, no accounts, no wallet connection. it cannot spend anything because it never asks for a key.
